Nova 2084 Animated Series Info

Nova 2084 is an ambitious, animated adult dystopian sci-fi series in current development by a group of young adults.

Originally once mentioned as a simple meme by the main creator, Novaur, Nova 2084 over the years has grown much much more and is shaping up to become a seriously ambitious animated series project, now in current development.

As of now, Nova 2084 is only just getting started in terms of development progress. The pilot script and overall a general idea for the full series' story is about finished. However, there's still much more to be done, such as finalizing the writing, storyboarding, production, marketing, etc. There's a LOT that goes into making this project we dream of a reality.

With all that being said, we very much can't wait for you to experience this original, dark, reality-breaking story, inspired by our love of Orwellian dystopian fiction and more!
