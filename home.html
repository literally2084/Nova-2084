Nova 2084 is a dystopian sci-fi animated series in development by Novaur, StellarSky, and a group of young adult friends. On January 1st, 2084, everything of a once free society is a faded memory. Individualism, if not

the matter of reality itself has been for decades tightly controlled by a mysterious, totalitarian rule. More than 90% of humanity is gone. Society is ranked and judged based on a culture of compliance and forced happiness.

Ideology is dead, everyone owns nothing, and are all mandated to be equipped with Augmented Reality (AR) mindchips. THE VISIONARY IS WATCHING. In this new dark era, a woman, Nova-84, unintentionally gets tangled into a group

of unknown rebels & hackers who plot to uncover the truth about the state of society, rebel against the global regime, and unblind the people from it's deep, nightmarish control.
